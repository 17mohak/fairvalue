# Roadmap

The single authoritative plan for FairValue. Part 1 is the research, which is locked. Part 2 is the
product, organised in sprints. Update the status columns after meaningful work, and record the
reasoning in `DECISIONS.md` and the facts in `CHANGELOG.md`.

_Last updated: 2026-11-05 · active branch `feat/multipage-experience` · nothing deployed_

Status values: **Done** (verified, with evidence), **Partial**, **Blocked** (a named external
blocker), **Open** (not started), **Won't do** (with the reason).

## Standing rules
- The research design is locked (`docs/RESEARCH_DESIGN.md`, D-011). The product never retrains,
  re-runs cross-validation or changes metrics or residual definitions. Research outputs are the
  source of truth.
- Never invent player data, portraits, shirt numbers, predictions or explanations. A missing asset
  shows an honest fallback.
- Nationality is never a model input. It is shown only where the audit context explains it.
- Pushing, merging into `main`, publishing images and public deployment each need the owner's
  explicit approval.

#### S2-01: Published research results for the showcase in the full export (P0, S)
- **Now / evidence:**
  - The full export has 0 players with a published explanation and 0 with a cached served
    estimate (counted in `players.json`). The eight-player showcase file has both.
  - `verify_players` compares the showcase with the full export, but not these fields.
- **Problem:** switching to the full export silently removed:
  - the Overview cover valuation, which renders only for a player with a published explanation;
  - every offline Valuation Journey, including the eight story players;
  - the match between stories and dossiers: the Todibo story shows published SHAP, while his
    dossier offline shows none.
- **Change:** `src.product.players` copies the published explanation and served estimate of the
  showcase players from the same sources the showcase uses (`reports/explainability`). No new
  computation.
- **User impact:** the front page regains its centrepiece, and stories and dossiers agree with or
  without the API.
- **Acceptance:**
  - A new verifier check finds the 8 profiles' explanations identical to the showcase.
  - The cover valuation renders with the full export.
  - Todibo's dossier, offline, shows the journey labelled "cached research result".
  - 25/25 checks pass.

#### S2-02: Overview spectrum axis (P0, S)
- **Now / evidence:** `web/src/app/page.tsx` fixes the axis at €200k–€80M ("wide enough for every
  showcase player"). With the full export the section shows the ten most valuable players
  (€130M–€200M):
  - every observed value is clamped to the right edge;
  - 8 of 10 Model A markers are hidden behind it.
- **Problem:** the chart draws ten identical rows; it reads as data but carries none.
- **Change:** take the domain from the plotted values plus a margin, keeping the tier zones and
  ticks. Re-check that "the ten most valuable" is the right selection, since the market-model gap
  is clearer across tiers.
- **User impact:** the Overview's first chart tells the truth.
- **Acceptance:** a test asserts that every plotted value lies inside the domain; a screenshot shows
  both markers on all rows.

#### S2-03: Portraits at scale (P0, L)
- **Now / evidence:**
  - The finder works with a contact (1.4).
  - Showcase: 4 of 8 players have an allowed licence and ≥ 1,400 px; Castro and Gueye are allowed
    but too small; Ignatov and Idrissi have no image. Full population: 3,674 allowed, of which 2,765
    are ≥ 800 px and only 1,755 are ≥ 1,400 px (1.4).
  - `add_portrait.py` requires a hand-made transparent cutout of at least 1,400 px per player.
- **Problem:** hand cutouts cannot scale beyond a few players, so the product stays imageless for
  almost everyone. Portraits are the owner's top priority.
- **Change (needs an owner decision):**
  - Add a second, honest rendering: a **framed photograph**. This is a licensed rectangular
    head-and-shoulders crop with an on-brand duotone or floodlight treatment and a visible credit,
    so no background removal is needed.
  - Keep cutouts for featured and story players.
  - Add a batch step: download allowed originals, propose a crop, write a review sheet, and add
    after human review.
  - Relax the 1,400 px minimum for framed crops to what the slot actually needs (about 800 px for
    2× displays).
  - CC BY-SA crops are derivatives: release the crop under the same licence and say it was
    cropped.
- **User impact:** real faces across the Explorer cards, dossier, Compare and Stories.
- **Acceptance:**
  - Every shown image is on the allow-list with author, licence, source and "cropped" or
    "background removed" credit, and was reviewed.
  - The coverage report counts portraits.
  - No layout shift; the same fallback everywhere.
  - Tests check the manifest against the files.
